---
title: "Search transfer"
description: "Query a transfer or withdrawal"
api: "GET /gateway/transfers-search"
---

Queries a transfer or withdrawal.

<Warning>
Avoid frequent polling. Use webhooks to track updates.
</Warning>

## Query parameters

<ParamField query="id" type="string">
  Withdrawal or transfer ID.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="identifier" type="string">
  Transfer identifier sent by your application.
</ParamField>

<ParamField query="status" type="string">
  Transfer status filter.
</ParamField>

## Request example

<RequestExample>

```bash
curl --request GET \
  --url 'https://app.amplopay.com/api/v1/gateway/transfers-search?identifier=identifier123' \
  --header 'x-public-key: SUA_CHAVE_PUBLICA_AQUI' \
  --header 'x-secret-key: SUA_CHAVE_PRIVADA_AQUI'
```

</RequestExample>
